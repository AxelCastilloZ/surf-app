# Proposal

## Why

The landing page lacks quick social-proof and trust signals that visitors can absorb without reading full sections. Value proposition badges (e.g., "ISA Certified", "500+ Students", "4.9 Google Rating", "#1 in Nosara") communicate credibility instantly and reduce bounce. These trust signals are standard in tourism/education sites and were noted as a gap in our competitor analysis.

## What Changes

- **Value badges strip**: A horizontal row of 4 compact badges placed just below the hero section, showing key trust signals with icons and short labels
- **Bilingual content**: Full ES/EN translations
- **Visual design**: Compact, icon-driven badges on a subtle background strip

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `ui/landing-value-badges`: Trust signal badges displayed below the hero section on the landing page

### Modified Capabilities

_None._

## Impact

- **Files modified:** `apps/web/src/pages/LandingPage.tsx`
- **Risk:** Low — adds a new visual strip, no changes to existing sections
- **No dependency changes**
